# 90-Day Checklist

Copy this into your task tracker, or tick it off here.

## Before day 1
- [ ] Business model, regulators and leadership team researched
- [ ] Mandate agreed: reporting line, budget, authority, success criteria
- [ ] Interview guide prepared

## Days 1–30: Listen and learn
- [ ] Incident readiness checked: IR plan, retainer, insurance, restore test, decision authority
- [ ] Break-glass and emergency contacts in hand
- [ ] Introduction email sent
- [ ] One-to-ones held with every team member
- [ ] 20+ stakeholder interviews completed and themes synthesised
- [ ] Existing documents collected (policies, risks, audits, budget, contracts, incidents)
- [ ] Crown jewels and critical services drafted
- [ ] Day-30 observations shared with your manager

## Days 31–60: Assess and prioritise
- [ ] Current-state assessment scored, with evidence
- [ ] Baseline metrics captured
- [ ] Top 10 risks agreed with business owners
- [ ] 3–5 quick wins chosen, delivered and communicated
- [ ] Draft target state defined

## Days 61–90: Plan and deliver
- [ ] Strategy on a page written
- [ ] Roadmap (Now / Next / Later) costed, with options
- [ ] Governance agreed: policies, risk acceptance, reporting rhythm
- [ ] Team structure and hiring plan drafted
- [ ] Executive team endorsement obtained
- [ ] Board or committee briefed
- [ ] Your 12-month objectives agreed with your manager
