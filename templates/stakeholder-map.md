## Influence and attitude grid

Place each stakeholder in a quadrant and plan accordingly:

| | **Champion / Neutral** | **Sceptic** |
|---|---|---|
| **High influence** | **Partner:** co-design the strategy and ask them to sponsor initiatives | **Engage first:** understand their objections, find shared goals, and remove friction for their teams |
| **Lower influence** | **Mobilise:** invite them to be security champions | **Keep informed:** show quick wins that help them |

## Relationship rhythm (after day 90)

| Stakeholder | Cadence |
|---|---|
| Your manager | Weekly |
| CFO, General Counsel, CIO/CTO | Monthly one-to-one |
| Audit / risk committee chair | Before each committee meeting |
| Business unit heads | Quarterly |
