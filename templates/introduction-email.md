# Introduction Email (Week 1)

Send it from your own account, ideally with your manager's endorsement. Keep it under 250 words, friendly and practical.

---

**Subject:** Hello from your new [CISO / Head of Security]

Hi everyone,

I joined [Organisation] this week as [title], reporting to [manager]. I'm responsible for helping us protect our customers, our people and our business from cyber threats, and for making security something that helps you get work done rather than getting in your way.

**My first few weeks:** I'll mostly be listening. I'll be meeting leaders and teams across the business to understand what you do, what worries you, and where security causes friction today. If you'd like to talk, just book 30 minutes in my calendar. I'd genuinely like to hear from you.

**Three things that help right now:**
1. **Report anything suspicious** to [security contact / report button]. There's no such thing as a silly report, and you'll never get in trouble for reporting.
2. **Use multi-factor authentication** wherever it's offered.
3. **Ask before you install.** If you need a new tool, including an AI tool, ask [IT / security] and we'll help you find a safe way.

**How I work:** I'd rather hear about a problem early than late. I'll be open about what we're doing and why, and I'll share what I learn from these conversations in about a month.

Looking forward to meeting you,

[Name]
[Title] · [email] · [phone]
