# 90-Day Board Briefing

*Format: 6 slides or a 2-page memo. Plan 15 minutes of presentation and 15 minutes of discussion. Pre-read sent 5 days ahead.*

---

## Slide 1 — Bottom line

- **Overall:** [one sentence, e.g. "Our foundations are better than expected, but we are exposed to a prolonged ransomware outage."]
- **Already improved:** [one or two quick wins, with numbers]
- **The ask:** [e.g. "Approve £X over two years for the resilience programme, and confirm our risk appetite for downtime."]

## Slide 2 — What I did

- Met [N] people across [N] teams, including [key customers / auditors / insurer]
- Assessed maturity across six areas using evidence, not just interviews
- Agreed the top 10 risks with their business owners

## Slide 3 — What I found

| Strengths | Gaps |
|---|---|
| [e.g. engaged engineering teams; EDR on 98% of laptops] | [e.g. backups not isolated from the network; no 24/7 monitoring] |

Maturity: [simple chart of the six function scores, today vs target]

## Slide 4 — Top risks

| Risk (business terms) | Impact | Within appetite? | Owner | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ransomware stops [operations] | [~£X per day] | No | [COO] | Initiative 2 |
| Compromise of [critical supplier] | [...] | No | [CPO] | Initiative 4 |
| [...] | | | | |

## Slide 5 — The plan

[Roadmap in three horizons (Now / Next / Later) with 4–6 initiatives, costs and the outcome of each.]

**Options:**

| Option | Cost | Risk remaining |
|---|---|---|
| A: Minimum | £ | [high risks remain: ...] |
| **B: Recommended** | £ | [residual risk within appetite by ...] |
| C: Accelerated | £ | [faster, higher delivery risk] |

## Slide 6 — Decisions and how you'll hear from me

**Decisions requested today:**
1. [...]
2. [...]

**Reporting from now on:** a quarterly report with 5 key risk indicators, top risks and progress, plus immediate notification of any SEV-1 incident.

---

## Tips

- Every risk owner has seen their risk **before** the meeting.
- No acronyms, no CVE numbers, no tool names on the slides.
- Be candid about bad news, and present it alongside the plan to fix it.
- Bring a one-page appendix with metric definitions in case you're asked.
- The [ciso-toolkit](https://github.com/Cyhelm-Vciso/ciso-toolkit) `board-report` skill can turn your notes and metrics into this format.
