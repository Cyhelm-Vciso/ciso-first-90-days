## How to announce a quick win

> **What changed:** All administrator accounts now require security-key MFA (up from 62% last month).
> **Why it matters:** Stolen admin passwords are one of the most common routes into ransomware attacks. This closes that route.
> **Thanks to:** the identity team and the 40 admins who enrolled in two weeks.
> **What's next:** extending to finance users in Q1.
