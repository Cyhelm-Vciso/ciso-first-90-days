# Stakeholder Interview Guide

**Format:** 30–45 minutes, one-to-one, in person or on video. Take notes, and tell people you'll share themes, not attributed quotes.

## Opening (2 min)

> "I'm new and I'm here to listen, not to judge. I want to understand your world, what worries you, and where security helps or gets in the way. I'll share the overall themes back with everyone in a few weeks."

## Core questions (ask everyone)

1. **Your role:** what are your top priorities for the next 12 months?
2. **Crown jewels:** what information, systems or processes would hurt most if they were stolen, altered or unavailable for a week?
3. **Worries:** what security risk worries you most, and why?
4. **Friction:** where does security slow you down or get in your way today?
5. **History:** has anything gone wrong before (incidents, near misses, audit findings)? What happened afterwards?
6. **Suppliers:** which third parties or tools are critical to your work?
7. **Change:** what's coming up (new products, markets, systems, acquisitions, AI projects) that I should know about early?
8. **Advice:** if you were me, what would you do first?
9. **People:** who else should I talk to?

## Extra questions by audience

**CEO / board members**
- How much risk are we willing to take to grow? Where is the line?
- How would you like to hear from me: format, frequency, and level of detail?
- What would make you confident the security programme is working?

**CFO**
- How is security funded today, and how are investments decided?
- What is our cyber insurance cover, and what did the insurer ask about at renewal?
- What would a week-long outage cost us?

**General Counsel / compliance / DPO**
- Which regulations and contractual commitments carry the biggest exposure?
- What are our incident notification obligations, and who decides on them?
- Are there open regulatory findings or disputes involving data?

**CIO / CTO / engineering**
- Where is the technical debt that worries you?
- How do changes get to production? Who can deploy?
- What would you stop doing if you could?

**HR**
- How do joiners and leavers work in practice? How quickly is access removed?
- How is security training perceived?

**Sales / customer success**
- Which customer security questions or deals have been hard? Have we lost deals over security?
- What have we promised customers about security?

**Your security team**
- What are you proud of? What frustrates you?
- What work would you stop, start or keep?
- What do you need from me?

## After each interview

Record in the [stakeholder map](stakeholder-map.md): their priorities, worries, influence, attitude to security, and any follow-ups. Send a thank-you note within 24 hours.

## Synthesis (end of week 4)

Group your notes into themes (for example "nobody owns supplier risk" or "deploys bypass review"). For each theme, record how many people raised it and the supporting evidence. Share the top 5–7 themes with your manager and the people you interviewed.
